SGP (Sintering Gas Plant) Spares & Equipment Replacement

Sintering Gas Plants (SGP) and Gas Cleaning Plants (GCP) handle highly abrasive, hot sintering dust and exhaust gases. SGP equipment spares—including dust collecting pipelines, screw conveyors, dampers, and expansion joints—are subject to continuous wear and chemical attack from sulfur oxides. Excellors specializes in custom SGP equipment replacement, engineering heavy-duty dust collection screw feeders, sector gates, and gas duct casings designed to prolong service life.

By combining high-chrome overlays, structural steel reinforcements, and positive seal alignments, our replacement spares reduce wear-induced air leaks and minimize maintenance shutdowns in SGP ducting paths.

Technical Specifications

  • Gas Duct Bellows Sizing: DN 50 mm to 8000 mm diameter metallic or rectangular fabric joints.
  • Exhaust Ratings: Rated for operational gas temperatures up to 350°C and 2.5 bar pressure range.
  • Screw Feeder flights: Fabricated standard or variable pitch flights with high-chrome chromium carbide overlay (CCO) wear plates.
  • Grate Bars & Plates: Coated sand cast Cr-Ni heat-resistant alloy steel (resists oxidation up to 1000°C).
  • Sector Gates: Fabricated dust-tight body plates supporting manual, hydraulic, or pneumatic cylinders.
  • Pulley Idlers: Precision-cast pulley rollers with excellent wear resistance and positive belt bite.
