Bespoke Heavy Fabrication

Industrial equipment subjected to high wear, hot environments, and abrasive materials requires advanced metallurgy, precision weld profiles, and rugged mechanical design.

At our manufacturing facilities, we translate engineering drawings into industrial-grade machinery. We specialize in custom steel fabrication of heavy bins, high-volume hopper assemblies, blast furnace burden tanks, refractory-lined tuyere stocks, insulated blowpipes, water-cooled elbows, coal injection lances, and custom alloy wear-linings. Every piece of equipment undergoes strict ultrasonic and visual testing before dispatch.

Our Manufacturing Process

1. Design & Detail

We use advanced CAD/CAM systems to detail custom wear configurations, ensuring compatibility with your existing mechanical systems.

2. Precision Cutting

Heavy plates are cut using high-accuracy CNC plasma units, allowing clean bevels and strict compliance with dimensional tolerances.

3. Weld & Assembly

Assembled by certified structural welders. We use high-grade MIG/TIG processes, ensuring excellent penetration and joint durability.

4. Quality Control

Non-destructive testing (NDT), ultrasonic testing of structural joints, and sandblasting/painting to specification.
