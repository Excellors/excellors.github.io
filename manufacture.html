Bespoke Heavy Fabrication, CAD Design & Testing

Industrial equipment subjected to high wear, hot environments, and abrasive materials requires advanced metallurgy, precision weld profiles, and rugged mechanical design.

At our manufacturing facilities, we translate engineering drawings into industrial-grade machinery. Using advanced versions of **AutoCAD, NX, and CAESAR II** for mechanical calculations of project products and pipeline design, we verify the structural integrity of our equipment. **Finite Element Analysis (FEA)** is also utilized in our calculations to ensure structural strength under load.

Our Manufacturing Process & Quality Control

1. Design & Detail

We use advanced CAD/CAM systems (AutoCAD, NX, CAESAR II) and FEA to detail custom wear configurations, ensuring compatibility with your existing mechanical systems.

2. Precision Cutting

Heavy plates are cut using high-accuracy CNC plasma units, allowing clean bevels and strict compliance with dimensional tolerances.

3. Weld & Assembly

Assembled by certified structural welders. We use high-grade MIG/TIG processes, ensuring excellent penetration and joint durability.

4. Testing Facilities

Complete Non-destructive testing (**NDT**) options: **Dolly, PT (Penetrant Testing), RT (Radiographic Testing), UT (Ultrasonic Testing), and RTD** checks, done in-house and on-site.
