BLT Distribution Chutes & Liners

The material distribution chute is a highly critical component of the Blast Furnace (BF) Top charging system. The reliability, longevity, and continuous availability of this equipment are of utmost importance for the smooth operation and optimal burden distribution inside the furnace.

Excellors manufactures high-performance distribution chutes customized to meet precise customer specifications. Engineered with high-temperature resistant steel bodies and lined with premium high chrome alloy wear-resistant tiles and high carbide overlays, our chutes offer exceptional resistance to severe abrasion and high-temperature environments.

Our chutes are designed for easy refurbishment and re-usability, providing a cost-effective wear-protection solution that reduces plant downtime and maintenance overheads.

Key Technical Specifications

  • Average Life Time: Highly durable lining providing a service life of about 18 to 24 months.
  • Length & Dimension: Fully customized to match any blast furnace throat layout.
  • High-Temp Body: Manufactured using specialized high-temperature resistant structural steel.
  • Wear Liners: High chrome alloy and high carbide overlay tiles for maximum abrasion protection.
  • Easy Refurbishment: Segmented design allowing swift replacement of worn tiles, enabling very easy re-usability.

Manufacturing & Delivery

Quality Standards: All chutes undergo rigorous dimensional inspection, structural alignment testing, and non-destructive testing (NDT) of welds before delivery.

Delivery Timeline: Standard manufacturing and delivery timeline of 3 months from design sign-off.

Product Details

  • Materials High Chrome Alloy, High Carbide Overlay
