Slag Storage Silos

Our slag storage silos are precision-engineered to meet the rigorous demands of modern industrial operations, offering a durable, weather-resistant solution for storing steel slag, blast furnace slag, and other bulk by-products.

Constructed from high-quality galvanized or stainless steel, these silos are built to withstand harsh environmental conditions, corrosive materials, and high-volume usage. The conical-bottom design ensures efficient material flow and easy integration with dewatering systems or automated discharge equipment, minimizing downtime and maintenance.

Key Structural Features

  • Anti-Corrosive Coating: Specialized double-epoxy liners to protect against acidic slag runoff.
  • Conical-Bottom Design: 60-degree steep discharge cone preventing material bridging.
  • Pneumatic Discharge Gates: High-performance dual-cylinder gates for regulated truck loading.
  • Integrated Safety Vents: Pressure relief valve assemblies to prevent implosions during high-speed vacuum discharge.

Project Challenges & Solutions

Challenge: The wet nature of blast furnace slag led to consistent material packing and gate blockage in the customer's previous silo designs.

Solution: Excellors designed a customized vibrating fluidization assembly above the discharge flange. The newly engineered gate system operates with hydraulic override triggers, allowing operators to break packed bridges safely without manual hammering.
