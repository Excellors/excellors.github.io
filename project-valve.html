High-Pressure Blast Furnace Gas Valves & DN 300 Goggle Valves

Blast furnace gas control systems require heavy-duty valve assemblies designed to isolate or regulate the flow of toxic, dusty, and flammable crude blast furnace gas. Due to high working temperatures and abrasive dust particles, these valves must be engineered for complete tightness, reliable seal separation, and quick mechanical actuation.

Excellors designs, manufactures, and supplies custom-engineered industrial valves, and specializes in the professional refurbishment and overhauling of large-scale plant isolation valves, including Upper Bank Valves and heavy slide gate valves. Our Goggle Valves, large-diameter slide gates, and refurbished assemblies feature robust bellows, structural support rods for clamping, and premium sealing rings to handle crude BF gas at temperatures up to 300°C.

Key Technical Specifications

  • Upper Bank Valve Refurbishment: Complete overhaul, valve seat grinding, seal replacement, and pressure safety checks.
  • Hydrostatic & Pressure Testing: Shop pressure testing using calibrated analog gauges to verify zero leakage.
  • Crude BF Gas Isolation: Engineered for hazardous media with working pressures up to 0.2 MPa.
  • Thermal Durability: Rated for operational temperatures up to 300°C with silicone/EPDM seals.
  • Gas-Tight Mechanical Seal: Lever clamping mechanism preventing ambient gas leakage.
  • Robust Structural Flanges: Thick multi-hole flanges for secure connection to gas ducts.

Design & Performance Advantages

By utilizing high-grade expansion bellows and a simplified manual or motorized clamping layout, our valves minimize maintenance intervals in high-dust gas routing. Every newly manufactured and refurbished valve undergoes hydrostatic and pneumatic pressure holding tests at our testing station, monitored via calibrated gauges, to certify zero leakage at operating pressures.
