Who We Are

Delivering Manufacturing & Field Services for Heavy Industry

Excellors Engineering Solutions Private Limited is a specialized engineering service and custom component fabrication firm. We deliver complete, turnkey solutions for bulk material handling equipment, industrial plant ducting, valves, and heavy structural systems.

Our core competence lies in planning, engineering, manufacturing, and executing complex plant shutdowns where timeline adherence and strict quality control are paramount. We work side-by-side with plant operations to increase safety, reduce component wear, and cut unplanned maintenance cycles.

Our Core Values

  • Integrity & Professionalism: Rigorous calculations, reliable scheduling, and transparent execution.
  • Uncompromised Safety Standards: Operating with active risk-assessments during heavy rigging and erection.
  • Agility & Speed: Swift response times and 24/7 technical mobilization during critical plant shutdowns.

Corporate Mission

To supply steel, cement, and power companies with engineered components that outlast standard OEM specifications, and install them safely with minimal disruption to operations.

Why Heavy Industries Choose Excellors

  • Experienced, certified plant-level engineers & technicians.
  • High-quality component fabrication with CNC precision.
  • Proven shutdown project speed to avoid costly commercial delays.
  • Bespoke modifications tailored to solve site wear-and-tear challenges.
  • Full compliance with structural regulations and safety clearances.
