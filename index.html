Quality Assurance

Engineered to Withstand Extreme Operational Stress

At Excellors, every manufactured structure and field joint is treated as safety-critical. We operate under rigorous non-destructive testing (NDT) standards to ensure structural integrity under dynamic plant load patterns.

  • Ultrasonic Weld Inspections: Full verification of major penetration welds on Hoppers and Burden Tanks.
  • Certified Erection Rigging: Rigging drawings and load margins reviewed by safety engineers before hoisting.
  • Alloy Grade Certification: Verification of wear-liner hardness properties (Hardox/stainless) before assembly.

Zero-Defect Quality Policy

Certified NDT inspections on all fabricated units.
