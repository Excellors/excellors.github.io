Heavy Industrial Material Hoppers & Burden Tanks

Heavy material hoppers are designed to receive, store, and discharge highly abrasive bulk materials like iron ore, sinter, and coal. They operate under massive dynamic impact loads and require structural reinforcements and wear-resistant liners to ensure long-term durability without structural deformation.

Excellors manufactured and supplied heavy-capacity receiving hoppers for Jindal Ferrous Limited at Jajpur, Odisha. Engineered from high-tensile structural steel, these hoppers feature segmented wear liner plates and external stiffeners to absorb dynamic impact.

Key Technical Specifications

  • Jindal Ferrous Compliance: Custom fabricated to meet Jajpur plant's precise space and hoisting constraints.
  • Heavy Reinforcement Ribs: External stiffeners preventing plate deflection under full material weight.
  • Wear Resistant Lining: Bolt-on chromium carbide overlay (CCO) wear plates for easy replacement.
  • High-Tensile Flange Welds: Penetration structural welds certified via NDT ultrasonic testing.

Manufacturing & Logistics Challenge

Challenge: Fabricating massive hopper cones that exceed standard highway clearance limits when pre-assembled at our Gangavathi workshop.

Solution: Cones were fabricated in modular, precision-milled segments. The final alignment check was performed in our shop using temporary jig setups, after which the modules were safely transported to Jajpur, Odisha, and welded on-site under strict QA monitoring.
