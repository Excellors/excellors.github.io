Blast Furnace Shutdowns, Overhauls & Maintenance

Blast furnace and steel manufacturing shutdowns require rapid, highly coordinated maintenance, mechanical overhauling, and software commissioning cycles. With down-time costs running extremely high, Excellors delivers expert site engineering squads, specialized tooling, and NDT inspection crews to execute critical maintenance cycles on-schedule.

We provide end-to-end technical support for shutdowns, including structural overhauling, replacement of worn refractory linings, mechanical re-alignment of drive units, and logic integration of process control interlocks.

Technical Specifications

  • Rapid Mobilization: Under 48 hours for emergency shutdown dispatch of core mechanical/software teams.
  • Rigging & Erection Codes: Adheres to AWS D1.1 structural welding and ASME Section IX pressure boundary codes.
  • Mechanical Alignment Tolerances: Laser shaft alignment down to ≤0.05 mm, coupling gaps ≤0.02 mm.
  • Non-Destructive Testing (NDT): Onsite dye penetrant testing (PT), ultrasonic testing (UT), radiographic testing (RT) verification.
  • PLC Logic Commissioning: Siemens S7-1500/PCS7, ABB 800xA, Honeywell Experion, Allen-Bradley Logix platforms.

Proven Project References

Since 2021, Excellors has successfully completed shutdown repair, erection, overhauling, and logic commissioning for major metallurgical plants in India:

  • TATA Steel: Equipment overhauling and replacement.
  • JSW Steel: Structural replacements and alignment services.
  • Jai Balaji Industries: Erection, Technical and Automation support for BF#1 (1x350m³) commissioning.
  • Kirloskar Ferrous Industries: Erection & commissioning updates on BF#1 & BF#2.
  • Bengal Energy Ltd & Indus Energy: Overhauls and valve automation sequencing.
